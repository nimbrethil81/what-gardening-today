# 🌱 What Gardening Today?

Gardening jobs for UK gardens, matched to the time of year, local weather and the
plants, tools and structures you have added.

[Open What Gardening Today?](https://whatgardeningtoday.com)

Sign in with Google to get started. Public sign-up is open; no invitation is needed.

- Choose what is in your garden from the catalogue.
- See seasonal jobs with practical instructions.
- Tick off completed jobs, or hide jobs you do not want to see.

An internet connection is needed to sign in and load or update your garden and jobs.

## Add to your iPhone home screen

1. Open [the app](https://whatgardeningtoday.com) in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new home-screen icon.

## About the app

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Supabase for sign-in and garden data.
The primary address is served by a Cloudflare Worker. The older
[GitHub Pages address](https://nimbrethil81.github.io/what-gardening-today/) is a legacy surface.
