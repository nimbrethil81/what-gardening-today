Dan Birchwood, who runs What Gardening Today?, is the data controller. I am an individual in the United Kingdom, and this is a personal project rather than a company.

Email whatgardeningtoday@gmail.com about anything on this page, including any of the rights below.

About the record of app opens

The app keeps a simple daily tally: this garden was opened today, this many times. It does not record which screens you looked at or what you did while it was open.

I use it to understand whether people come back, and whether the app is worth continuing to maintain. Answering that requires recognising that the same garden was opened on different days, which is why it is kept at all. It is not used to profile you, to advertise to you, or to make any decision about you, and it is not used for anything beyond the purposes described in this notice.

Why the app uses this information

  • To provide the service you asked for — your account, your garden and its location and contents, the jobs you complete or hide, and everything needed to work out what to suggest. It also covers sending a postcode or coordinates to postcodes.io to find or check a garden's location, and sending an approximate location to OpenWeather to get the weather. (Performance of a contract.)
  • To keep the app secure and working — sign-in times, and the limited technical information involved in serving the website and running the database. My interest is in preventing misuse, investigating faults, and protecting people's accounts. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To understand whether the app is useful — the daily tally of app opens described above. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To read and reply to messages — feedback and emails you send, along with your email address and the app version. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To handle data protection requests and complaints — including keeping a record that I dealt with one. (Legal obligation.)

You are not required by law to give me any of this. To have an account you must sign in with Google, and to get gardening suggestions you must tell the app where the garden is and what is in it. Sending feedback is entirely optional.

Your right to object

Where the app relies on legitimate interests — including the daily tally of app opens — you can object to it. Email whatgardeningtoday@gmail.com and say what you are objecting to. I will stop unless there is a compelling reason not to, and for the tally I would expect to simply stop.

What is stored on your device

Three things, and nothing else:

  • Your sign-in, so you stay signed in between visits. The app cannot work signed-in without it.
  • A copy of the app itself, to help its screens load on a poor connection. Your garden data and today's suggestions still need a connection.
  • Which garden you last looked at, so it opens where you left off. This affects only how the app appears on that device, and you can turn it off in Settings.

There is no advertising storage, no analytics storage, and nothing that follows you to other websites. These uses fall within the exceptions in the rules on storing things on your device, so the app does not show a cookie banner. The daily tally of app opens is recorded on the server, not on your device.

These are the services involved in running the app.

Coordinates sent for this check are not rounded to the coarse weather location. A free UK service run by Ideal Postcodes.

Where that happens I rely on the safeguards in each provider's data processing agreement, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where it applies.

Ask me at whatgardeningtoday@gmail.com if you would like more detail about a particular transfer or the safeguards behind it.

How long it is kept

Your account: for as long as it exists. Garden records: for as long as the garden exists.

You can delete your account yourself, from Settings inside the app. A successful deletion removes your account, your garden memberships and any feedback you sent through the app from the live database straight away. It cannot be undone through the app.

A garden where you are the only member is deleted too, including its contents, job history, hidden jobs and daily activity records. If other members remain, the garden and those records stay available to them, including things you added or recorded. If you were its only owner, ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member. Please check what will happen before you confirm.

Deleted information may survive for a short time in access-restricted backups of the database, for up to seven days, until those backups expire in the ordinary course. They exist to recover from a disaster, not to keep a copy of you, and they are not searched or used for anything else.

Emails and other messages you send me directly live in my email account and are not deleted by deleting your app account. I keep them for up to 24 months after our last exchange, and then delete them — unless one is still needed to deal with a complaint, a security problem or a legal claim. Ask me at any time and I will delete correspondence sooner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to:

  • tell you what the app holds about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information — though deleting your account yourself, in Settings, is quicker;
  • restrict how it is used;
  • provide it in a portable form;
  • stop using it where the app relies on legitimate interests — see Your right to object above.

Email whatgardeningtoday@gmail.com. I will respond without undue delay and normally within one month. If the law allows longer because a request is complicated, I will tell you inside that first month. There is normally no charge.

Complaints

If you think I have handled your information badly, you can complain to me by emailing whatgardeningtoday@gmail.com. I will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it, and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, at any time and whether or not you have contacted me first: ico.org.uk, or 0303 123 1113.

Security

The app stores no passwords — signing in is handled entirely by Google. Access rules are enforced in the database itself as well as in the app, and are designed so that you can reach only gardens you are a member of. Connections are encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure, and this one is run by one person as a hobby. So a practical suggestion rather than a legal one: please don't put anything sensitive into garden names, plant nicknames or feedback messages.

Children

The app is for adults, and you must be 18 or over to create an account. If I find out that somebody under 18 has created one, I will remove the account and its information.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and I will let existing users know, either directly or in the app. Small corrections happen without ceremony.
