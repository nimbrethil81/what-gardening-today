1. What this is

What Gardening Today? is a free web app that suggests gardening jobs for the plants, tools and structures you tell it you have, adjusted for the time of year and the weather where your garden is. It is run by Dan Birchwood, an individual in the United Kingdom, as a personal project rather than a business.

It is written for gardens in the United Kingdom. The growing calendar, the weather rules and the plant list are all UK-specific, and the advice will not be right anywhere else.

2. It is guidance, not professional advice

This matters more than anything else here, so it is not buried in a list.

The app gives general gardening guidance. It is not professional horticultural, arboricultural, agricultural, veterinary or medical advice, and it is not a substitute for any of those.

The app does not know your garden. It knows what you have typed into it. It cannot see the condition of a tree, the state of a wall, how heavy a job is, whether a plant is diseased, whether something near it is poisonous, or whether you are able to do safely what it suggests. You are the one who can see the garden, and the final judgement is always yours.

In particular, please use your own judgement about:

  • Anything involving height, ladders, power tools or sharp tools. If a job looks beyond you, it is — get somebody in.
  • Trees and large structures. Work on mature trees, walls and roofs may need a qualified person. Before working on a tree, check whether it is covered by a Tree Preservation Order or stands in a conservation area — either may mean you need your council's consent or must give them notice first. Check any restrictions from your landlord too.
  • Anything that might be eaten. Never eat a plant, fruit, seed, berry or fungus on the strength of this app. Many common garden plants are toxic to people, and others are toxic to cats, dogs and horses.
  • Pesticides and weedkillers. Use only a product authorised for home garden use, and for the job in question, and follow its label exactly. For other products, follow the manufacturer's instructions. Keep them away from children and animals.
  • Wildlife, hedges and nests. Before cutting a hedge or working on a tree, check for active birds' nests and for protected animals such as bats. A nest is protected whenever it is in use or being built — this does not depend on a fixed season, so checking matters at any time of year.
  • Invasive plants. Do not plant a controlled invasive species or allow one to spread into the wild, and check the official guidance before cutting, moving or disposing of one — disposal in particular is regulated.
  • Local restrictions. Protected trees, wildlife rules, hosepipe and water-use bans and some hedgerow rules vary by place, and differ across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The app does not know which apply to your garden.

If anything the app suggests seems wrong, unsafe, or does not match what you can see in front of you, trust the garden and not the app — and please tell me, so I can fix it.
